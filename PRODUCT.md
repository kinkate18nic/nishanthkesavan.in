# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients looking for a capable independent studio for web development, SEO, data products, and practical digital tools. Secondary visitors include collaborators, hiring teams, and people discovering Nishanth's public-interest projects. They need to understand quickly what Nishanth builds, how he thinks, and whether he can be trusted to finish and maintain real work.

## Product Purpose

Present Nishanth Kesavan as a resourceful independent builder who turns overlooked Indian problems into useful, maintained products. The site should make his unusual path from corporate data work to AI-assisted product building credible, show concrete evidence through live projects, and invite specific client or collaboration conversations.

## Brand Personality

Resourceful, precise, dependable. The voice is candid, observant, and quietly confident. It should feel like meeting a thoughtful builder with strong judgment, not reading a polished agency pitch.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dark developer portfolios, neon gradients, floating blobs, glass cards, emoji placeholders, generic card grids, startup slogans, inflated expertise claims, and copy that hides the role of AI. The site must not look template-generated or resemble a fashionable SaaS landing page.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with useful work and the reasoning behind it.
2. Let specificity create credibility: name the problem, the decision, and the maintained outcome.
3. Present AI assistance honestly as part of a disciplined process, not as a novelty or apology.
4. Use restraint to signal precision, with a few memorable details that feel personal to Nishanth.
5. Make it easy for a prospective client to move from curiosity to a concrete conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus states, semantic structure, and touch targets. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid communicating status through color alone. Keep typography legible on small screens and on lower-quality displays.
